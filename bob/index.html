how your token is kept safe

Your OANDA token is locked in this browser with a key made from your login password, and only the locked copy is saved. Nothing else can open it: not the server, not the database, not Chris. When you press start, or sign in after the server restarted, your browser unlocks it and hands it to the server's running process, which keeps it in memory only. If you change your password, paste your token again.
